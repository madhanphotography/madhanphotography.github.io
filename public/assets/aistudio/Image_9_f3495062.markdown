## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the regal reverence, cultural profundity, and timeless intimacy of luxury South Indian heritage weddings. Designed specifically to frame sacred ceremonies—from the quiet dawn of the *Muhurtham* to the vivacity of the *Oonjal* and *Haldi*—the aesthetic draws inspiration from Dravidian temple architecture, gilded Kanjeevaram silks, brass oil lamps (*Kuthu Vilakku*), and fragrant sandalwood paste.

### Visual Style
The design blends **Classical Luxury Minimalist Editorial** with **Tactile Heritage Textures**:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Anchored on warm ivory, raw silk undertones, and aged temple gold, eschewing clinical white canvases.
- **Architectural Framing:** Subtle structural motifs honoring stepped temple sanctums (*Mandapam* pillars and thresholds), employing deliberate hairline framing rather than heavy boundaries.
- **Editorial Grandeur:** Generous negative space, large-format cinematic imagery, and classical high-contrast serif typography evoke high-end royal monographs and archival heirloom albums.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in auspicious ceremonial substances: *Kumkumam* (vermilion), *Pattu* (temple gold embroidery), and *Chandanam* (sandalwood paste).

- **Primary (`#8B1E28` — Deep Vermilion / Kumkum):** Serves as the primary brand anchor, focal accents, key interactive states, and editorial highlights. Evokes sacred intimacy and traditional silks.
- **Secondary (`#C59B4E` — Temple Gold / Brass):** Evokes temple bells, heirloom jewelry, and morning sunlight spilling through stone pillared corridors. Used for gilded dividers, bespoke badges, awards, and subtle framing.
- **Tertiary (`#4A1B22` — Deep Bronze Maroon):** A grounding, near-black umber-maroon hybrid used for high-contrast typography, formal captions, and deep-shadowed vignettes.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0` — Warm Sandalwood / Raw Silk):** The canvas base, establishing a soft, non-glare, heirloom paper-like foundation. Secondary neutral tiers descend to warm brass-washed tones (`#F0EAE1`) and deep antique charcoal (`#211A1B`) for text on neutral backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes literary, transitional serifs with a clean, understated humanist sans-serif to create an evocative gallery experience.

- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Ceremony Titles):** Provides majestic drama and classical Indian high-society dignity. High contrast between thick stems and razor-thin serifs elevates ceremony titles (*The Kanyadaanam at Brihadisvara*, *A Madurai Morning*).
- **EB Garamond (Editorial Body Text & Narrative Notes):** Imparts warmth, romance, and literary permanence to couple stories, vow excerpts, and ceremonial descriptions.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Labels, Metadata, & Action Elements):** Applied sparingly in uppercase with wide tracking for metadata (e.g., location, film lens specs, dates, button calls-to-action) to balance the elaborate serifs with crisp, contemporary navigation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an archival editorial layout with generous breathing room, reminiscent of limited-edition coffee-table monographs.

- **Grid Architecture:** A 12-column asymmetric fluid grid on desktop (max content boundary: 1440px) allowing staggered, uneven visual weights for wedding photojournalism (e.g., full-bleed portraits side-by-side with detail shots of jasmine garlands, antique temple jewelry, and *Akshata* grains). On tablet, transitions to an 8-column grid; on mobile, condenses to a 4-column flow.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Emphasizes deep vertical pauses between ceremonial chapters. Section gaps prioritize ample separation (`space-xl` and above) to let viewers absorb the emotion of each moment.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (single-column stories, edge-to-edge full bleed galleries, condensed margins)
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (balanced two-column pairs, medium gutters)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (multi-tier curation, immersive split-screen compositions)

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids modern synthetic drop shadows and plastic blur effects, opting instead for architectural, material-grounded elevation.

- **Warm Tonal Surfaces:** Layering is achieved by shifting background tones between the base raw silk ivory (`#F9F6F0`) and layered card mats in bleached parchment (`#FAF7F2`) or deep ceremonial vermilion (`#8B1E28`) for full-takeover modal showcases.
- **Gilded Hairline Filigree:** Cards, elevated photo frames, and interactive sheets are outlined with delicate 1px borders in antiqued gold at low opacity (`rgba(197, 155, 78, 0.28)`), providing structure without visual clutter.
- **Heirloom Diffusion Shadows:** When floating elements are necessary (e.g., wedding date pickers, inquiry overlays, lightbox navigation), use an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with deep burnt amber rather than neutral black: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(74, 27, 34, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary respects the formal, structural geometry of South Indian stone architecture, temple *gopurams*, and traditional framed family portraits.

- **Subtle Softness (Level 1):** Subtle corner radius (0.25rem / 4px) softens interactive buttons and form fields, avoiding modern bubbly pills while retaining the organic touch of handmade cotton paper and mounted photo albums.
- **Arched Heritage Frames:** Specialized visual containers (curated hero imagery, ceremonial showcase badges) may adopt a classic South Indian temple arch or scalloped threshold top profile, while retaining crisp structural vertical edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Inquire / Book Date):** Filled Deep Vermilion (`#8B1E28`) with warm ivory text (`#F9F6F0`), uppercase tracked labels (`label-lg`), 4px border radius. Hover introduces a subtle warm bronze glow with a gold hairline outline (`#C59B4E`).
- **Secondary Action (Explore Ceremony Stories):** Transparent background with a 1px border in aged gold (`#C59B4E`), primary vermilion text, shifting to a soft gold wash on hover.
- **Icon / Narrative Triggers:** Deep bronze circular controls with 1px gold borders, featuring fine serif arrows or traditional South Indian decorative motifs (e.g., stylized lotus buds, *diya* flame icons).

### Chips & Ceremony Tags
- Used for ceremony categorization (e.g., *Muhurtham*, *Oonjal*, *Sangeeth*, *Kanyadaanam*, *Vedic Rites*).
- Styled with an ivory background, subtle 1px border in `#C59B4E`, and tracked small-caps text (`label-sm`) in `#8B1E28`. Active states reverse with a solid vermilion fill and gold lettering.

### Photo Cards & Editorial Mounts
- Designed like museum mounts: ample inner ivory matte margins around high-resolution wedding captures.
- Hover reveals a quiet zoom and brings forth an overlay caption typed in italicized `EB Garamond` describing the cultural ritual.
- Micro-dividers use a double-hairline pattern: an inner solid line bordered by subtle gold dots or lotus flourishes.

### Form Inputs & Date Inquiries
- Single-line baseline borders or framed sand-colored boxes (`#F0EAE1`) topped with floating labels in uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- Focus state highlights with an illuminated gold underline (`#C59B4E`) and a slight vermilion accent dot.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with softened corners (2px) or circular forms styled with 1px antique bronze borders. Selected state fills with deep vermilion, accented by a warm gold interior checkmark.

### Ceremony Milestone Accordion
- A narrative accordion component tailored for wedding timelines (e.g., *Dawn Sankalpam* to *Evening Reception*), featuring gold roman numerals, serif headers, and expandable split-screen preview photos.
